Malta

Malta packs a lot into a small island — St Julian's and Paceville give it a proper nightlife scene, while the rest of the island is genuinely beautiful, from the fortified capital Valletta to the Blue Lagoon on Comino.

nightlife

Paceville, in St Julian's, is Malta's main nightlife hub — a dense cluster of bars and clubs that's the go-to base for most UK groups.

beaches

The Blue Lagoon on Comino is the standout, all clear turquoise water, usually reached by a boat trip. Closer to St Julian's, Sliema and Qawra have easy beach access without needing to travel far.

getting there

Flights from most UK airports typically take around 3 hours into Malta International Airport.

best time to go

July and August are the busiest and warmest months for nightlife. June and September offer a quieter alternative with still-warm weather.
