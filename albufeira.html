Albufeira

Albufeira is the Algarve's biggest party resort, on Portugal's southern coast. It splits neatly into two sides: the Old Town, with cobbled streets, seafood restaurants, and a more relaxed pace, and the Strip, a dense run of bars and clubs that's the main draw for UK groups.

nightlife

The Strip is where it happens — a concentrated stretch of bars and clubs that gets going late and runs into the early hours, especially popular with UK groups looking for a big night out.

beaches

Albufeira has around 25 beaches along its coastline, from central spots like Fisherman's Beach right by the Old Town to quieter coves further out. Falésia Beach, with its golden cliffs, is one of the standouts nearby.

getting there

Flights land at Faro Airport, roughly 2 hours 45 minutes from most UK airports, with a transfer to Albufeira of around 30-45 minutes.

best time to go

July and August are the busiest and warmest months. May, June, September, and October offer a quieter alternative with still-good weather.
