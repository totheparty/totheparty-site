Ayia Napa

Ayia Napa is Cyprus's biggest party resort, known for Nissi Beach by day and 'the Strip' by night. It has a reputation as one of the liveliest nightlife scenes in Europe.

nightlife

The Strip has a dense run of clubs and bars, with the summer season pulling in international DJs at some of the bigger venues.

beaches

Nissi Beach is the standout — golden sand, shallow clear water, and a lively daytime beach party scene of its own.

getting there

Flights typically run around 4.5 hours from the UK into Larnaca, then a short transfer to the resort.

best time to go

July and August are peak nightlife season with the highest temperatures. Late spring and early autumn are quieter.
