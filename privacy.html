privacy policy

last updated: 30 July 2026. this page explains how totheparty.co.uk ("we," "us," "the site") handles your data, uses cookies, and the terms under which you use this site. we're a small, independent business — if anything here is unclear, get in touch.

who we are

totheparty.co.uk is a UK-based holiday deals comparison website. we are not a travel agent, tour operator, or ATOL holder — we do not sell, book, or process payment for any holiday. all bookings are made directly with the third-party provider linked from each deal, and their own terms and privacy policy apply to that booking.

what data we collect

if you sign up for deal alerts, we collect your email address. we do not currently require an account to browse the site, and we don't collect payment information, since we don't process any bookings ourselves.

if we add analytics tools in future, we may also collect standard technical data such as your approximate location (from IP address), browser type, device type, and pages visited — used only to understand how the site is used, never to identify you personally.

how we use your data

your email address is used to send you holiday deal alerts and related updates, via our email provider, Klaviyo. you can unsubscribe at any time using the link in any email we send, or by contacting us directly.

we do not sell your data to third parties. we do not share your email address with the travel providers we link to — clicking a deal takes you to their site directly, and any data you share with them from that point is governed by their own privacy policy, not ours.

legal basis for processing (UK GDPR)

we process your email address on the basis of your consent, given when you sign up for deal alerts. you can withdraw this consent at any time by unsubscribing. we may process limited technical/analytics data on the basis of legitimate interest, to understand and improve how the site performs.

how long we keep your data

we keep your email address for as long as you remain subscribed to deal alerts, or until you ask us to delete it. if you unsubscribe, your email is removed from our active marketing list.

your rights

under UK GDPR, you have the right to: access the data we hold about you; ask us to correct or delete it; restrict or object to how we use it; and request a copy in a portable format. to exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details below. you also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk if you believe your data has been mishandled.

cookies

this site currently uses minimal cookies. third-party services we use (such as our email provider and, if enabled, analytics tools) may set their own cookies to support their functionality — for example, to remember that you've signed up, or to measure site usage anonymously. you can control or delete cookies at any time through your browser's settings. blocking cookies may affect how parts of the site function.

affiliate disclosure

totheparty.co.uk is an affiliate marketing website. when you click through to a travel provider from a deal listed on this site and go on to book, we may earn a commission — at no extra cost to you. this commission does not influence which deals we list or how we present prices, but it is how the site generates revenue and remains free to use.

accuracy of prices & deals

prices, availability, and deal details shown on this site are provided for guidance and can change at any time on the provider's own site, independent of us. always check the final price, dates, and terms directly with the provider before booking. we make reasonable efforts to keep listings accurate and up to date but cannot guarantee real-time accuracy at all times.

third-party links

this site links to third-party travel providers for the purpose of booking holidays. we are not responsible for the content, accuracy, availability, or practices of any third-party site linked from here, including their own privacy and cookie policies.

limitation of liability

we provide this site and its content on an "as is" basis. to the fullest extent permitted by law, we are not liable for any loss or damage arising from your use of this site or reliance on information shown on it, including inaccurate pricing or unavailable deals. this does not affect your statutory rights.

intellectual property

the design, branding, and original written content on this site belong to totheparty.co.uk. deal images and third-party logos (where used) remain the property of their respective owners.

children

this site is not directed at, or intended for use by, anyone under 18. we do not knowingly collect data from children.

governing law

this policy and your use of the site are governed by the laws of England and Wales.

changes to this policy

we may update this policy from time to time. the "last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent change. continued use of the site after an update means you accept the revised policy.

contact us

for any questions about this policy, your data, or to exercise your rights, contact us at: hello@totheparty.co.uk
