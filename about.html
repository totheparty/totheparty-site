about us

totheparty is a simple idea: the best party holiday deals, all in one place, without the fifty open tabs it usually takes to find them.

we check holidays across every major travel site so you don't have to — Zante, Ibiza, Magaluf, Ayia Napa, Kavos, Malia, and the other spots people actually go for a proper night out. we're not interested in family resorts or quiet countryside breaks. just the destinations built for a good time, at the lowest price we can find.

we're not a travel agent and we don't sell holidays ourselves. every deal links straight through to the actual provider — the airline, tour operator, or booking site — so you book directly with them, ATOL protection and all. we may earn a small commission when you book through one of our links, at no extra cost to you. it's how we keep the site free and the deals coming.

this site is a work in progress and growing fast. if there's a destination, feature, or deal you think we're missing, we'd genuinely love to hear about it.

we compare holidays across every major travel site, so you don't have to. real prices, updated daily, no fluff.
