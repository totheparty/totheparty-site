Ibiza

Ibiza is the original party island — home to some of the most famous superclubs in the world. San Antonio is where most UK party groups base themselves, while Ibiza Town and Playa d'en Bossa lean a bit more upmarket.

nightlife

San Antonio's Sunset Strip and West End are packed with bars, and the island's superclubs (Ushuaïa, Hï Ibiza, Pacha) host some of the biggest DJs in the world through summer.

beaches

Ibiza has some genuinely excellent beaches beyond the clubs — Cala Comte and Cala Bassa are known for clear water, while Playa d'en Bossa mixes beach clubs with actual sand time.

getting there

Flights run from most UK airports in summer, typically around 2.5 hours.

best time to go

June through September is peak clubbing season, when all the big clubs and events are running.
