Kavos

Kavos, on the southern tip of Corfu, has built its reputation as one of the liveliest party destinations from the UK — a compact strip that's especially popular with first-time groups.

nightlife

The main strip is dense with bars and clubs, with much of the action spilling onto the beach at spots like Atlantis into the early hours.

beaches

Kavos has a long stretch of sandy beach right by the action, plus quieter options like Arkoudilas a short drive away for a calmer day.

getting there

Flights run around 3 hours from most UK airports in peak season.

best time to go

Mid-June through August is peak season and busiest. May and early June are quieter.
