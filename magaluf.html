Magaluf

Magaluf is Majorca's answer to a full-on party resort — famously centred on 'the Strip,' a stretch of bars and clubs that's one of the most popular 18-30 destinations from the UK.

nightlife

The Strip is the centrepiece — wall-to-wall bars, pub crawls, and clubs like BCM. Punta Ballena runs parallel and is just as busy.

beaches

Magaluf beach itself is a proper sandy bay right in the centre of the action, good for recovering during the day before heading back out.

getting there

Very short flight times from the UK, often under 2.5 hours, with routes from almost every regional airport.

best time to go

July and August are peak season and busiest. June and September offer a quieter alternative with still-warm weather.
